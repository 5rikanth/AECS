MISSING PERSON RESPONSE PROTOCOL (Reference Knowledge Base — academic prototype)

Primary service: Police, for reports of a person (child, elderly relative,
family member, friend) who has not returned or cannot be located after an
expected time.

Severity indicators:
- CRITICAL: missing child, missing elderly person with a medical
  condition, or missing person last seen in a dangerous situation.
- HIGH: missing for an unusually long time relative to normal routine,
  or caller expresses strong concern for safety.
- MEDIUM: missing for a shorter/ambiguous duration, caller unsure if
  it's actually unusual.
- LOW: rarely applies to this category.
- UNKNOWN: no duration or context given about how unusual the absence is.

Notes: A missing child or missing elderly relative should default to at
least HIGH severity even with minimal detail — age/vulnerability is
itself a severity signal, not something to wait for further evidence on.
required_service is normally "police"; use "unknown" only if the
transcript is too vague to confirm this is actually a missing-person
report versus, e.g., someone simply being late.
