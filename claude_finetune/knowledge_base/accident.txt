ACCIDENT RESPONSE PROTOCOL (Reference Knowledge Base — academic prototype)

Primary service: Ambulance is the default response for any accident
involving injury, unconsciousness, bleeding, or a caller who reports pain.

Secondary service: Police should additionally be notified for hit-and-run
incidents, accidents blocking traffic with multiple vehicles, or when a
driver has fled the scene. Use "multiple" as required_service when both
apply.

Fire Brigade should be added instead of/alongside ambulance if the vehicle
has caught fire, fuel is leaking near an ignition source, or the caller
mentions smoke/fire at the accident site.

Severity indicators:
- CRITICAL: unconsciousness, heavy/uncontrolled bleeding, suspected spinal
  injury, entrapment inside the vehicle, multiple casualties.
- HIGH: broken bones, moderate bleeding, visible trauma, caller reports
  someone "cannot move" or "cannot get up".
- MEDIUM: pain reported but caller/victim is conscious, mobile, and
  describes the injury as manageable.
- LOW: minor scrapes, no reported pain beyond shock, vehicle damage only,
  no injury mentioned.
- UNKNOWN: no injury information given at all and no other severity cue.

Notes: If the transcript does not mention injuries, do not assume none
exist. An accident with zero injury/severity information should default
to ambulance dispatch as a precaution, with severity left UNKNOWN rather
than assumed low.
