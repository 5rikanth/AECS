DOMESTIC EMERGENCY RESPONSE PROTOCOL (Reference Knowledge Base — academic prototype)

This category covers emergencies occurring within the caller's own
household: domestic violence, serious family disputes turning physical,
child/elder abuse at home, and home-specific hazards (gas leak, electric
shock, kitchen fire) that are domestic in nature rather than a generic
fire/accident report.

Primary service: Police, for violence, abuse, or a dangerous dispute
between household members.

Ambulance should be used instead of (or alongside, as "multiple") police
when the transcript's primary content is a medical symptom (e.g. "saans
lene mein takleef" — breathing difficulty) even if it happens to occur at
home and the caller mentions being alone — a lone person having a medical
symptom at home is a MEDICAL EMERGENCY, not domestic violence, unless
another person's actions are described as the cause.

Fire Brigade / multiple should be used for gas leaks, electric shocks, or
kitchen fires at home.

Severity indicators:
- CRITICAL: active violence happening now, weapon involved, victim unable
  to move/breathe, electric shock with unconsciousness.
- HIGH: threat of imminent violence, gas leak actively smelled, injury
  from earlier violence reported.
- MEDIUM: verbal dispute escalating but not yet physical.
- LOW: dispute described as calming down / already resolved.
- UNKNOWN: no detail on what specifically is happening.

Notes: Do not classify a lone person's medical symptom (breathing
difficulty, chest pain, etc.) as domestic_emergency purely because they
mention being alone at home — check whether another person's action
caused the situation. If not, this is medical_emergency instead.
