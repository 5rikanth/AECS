TRAPPED PERSON RESPONSE PROTOCOL (Reference Knowledge Base — academic prototype)

Primary service: Rescue, for anyone trapped/stuck — elevator, debris,
flood water, a confined space, or behind a jammed door after an accident.

Fire Brigade should be used instead of (or alongside, as "multiple")
rescue when the trapping is inside a burning or structurally unstable
building — fire brigades typically handle structural extraction in many
jurisdictions.

Ambulance should be added ("multiple") whenever breathing difficulty,
injury, or prolonged entrapment (implying possible medical distress) is
mentioned.

Severity indicators:
- CRITICAL: breathing difficulty, rising water level, unstable/collapsing
  structure, or injury mentioned alongside being trapped.
- HIGH: trapped with no immediate life-threat mentioned but no way out
  and actively distressed.
- MEDIUM: trapped in a low-risk situation (e.g. a stalled elevator with
  no other danger mentioned).
- LOW: rarely applies to this category.
- UNKNOWN: "trapped" stated with zero further detail.

Notes: Being trapped is inherently urgent; do not let a lack of
additional detail lower severity below MEDIUM — use UNKNOWN only when
truly no context exists, not as a default "safe" assumption.
