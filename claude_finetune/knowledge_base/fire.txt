FIRE RESPONSE PROTOCOL (Reference Knowledge Base — academic prototype)

Primary service: Fire Brigade, for any confirmed fire, smoke, gas leak
with fire risk, or explosion (cylinder blast, short circuit fire).

Secondary service: Ambulance should be added ("multiple") whenever the
caller mentions being unable to breathe, being trapped inside, injuries,
or anyone still inside the burning structure — smoke inhalation and burns
are common in fire incidents even when not explicitly stated as "injury".

Police may be added if the fire appears to be arson/deliberate, or if
there's a crowd-control/evacuation concern mentioned.

Severity indicators:
- CRITICAL: caller or others trapped inside, cannot exit, heavy smoke,
  breathing difficulty due to smoke, explosion, fire spreading fast.
- HIGH: active fire confirmed but caller/occupants are outside and safe,
  fire still spreading.
- MEDIUM: small/contained fire, caller unsure how serious.
- LOW: fire already out or very minor (e.g. small kitchen flare-up
  explicitly described as controlled).
- UNKNOWN: only "smoke smell" or ambiguous mention, no confirmation.

Notes: A caller reporting fire AND that they personally cannot exit the
building should always be treated as CRITICAL with required_service
"multiple" (fire_brigade + ambulance), regardless of whether they
separately mention an injury.
