NATURAL DISASTER RESPONSE PROTOCOL (Reference Knowledge Base — academic prototype)

Primary service: Rescue, for flood, earthquake, landslide, storm damage,
or structural collapse caused by weather/natural events.

Use "multiple" (rescue + ambulance) whenever injuries or trapped people
are mentioned alongside the disaster.

Severity indicators:
- CRITICAL: people trapped in debris/water, structural collapse with
  people inside, rapidly rising water.
- HIGH: significant property damage described, ongoing dangerous
  conditions (e.g. water still rising, aftershocks).
- MEDIUM: damage reported but situation described as currently stable.
- LOW: minor damage, no danger to people described.
- UNKNOWN: event mentioned with no detail on current danger level.

Notes: Natural disaster reports mentioning any people still in danger
(trapped, in rising water, under debris) should always be CRITICAL
regardless of how the rest of the transcript is phrased.
