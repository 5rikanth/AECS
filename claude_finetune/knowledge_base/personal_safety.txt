PERSONAL SAFETY / THREAT RESPONSE PROTOCOL (Reference Knowledge Base — academic prototype)

Primary service: Police, for being followed, stalked, threatened,
harassed, or feeling generally unsafe due to another person's behavior,
where no injury or property crime has occurred yet.

Ambulance should be added only if the caller mentions an actual physical
injury already sustained.

Severity indicators:
- CRITICAL: caller says someone is trying to grab/attack them right now,
  or a weapon is mentioned.
- HIGH: active following/stalking in progress, explicit threats made,
  caller sounds scared and is currently in the situation ("abhi",
  "right now", present tense).
- MEDIUM: caller describes a threat that already ended, or vague unease
  without a specific ongoing action.
- LOW: caller mentions discomfort but explicitly says they are now safe.
- UNKNOWN: no severity cue at all.

Notes: An active "being followed right now" or "someone is stalking me"
report, by itself, should default to at least HIGH severity even with no
other detail — the ongoing/ present-tense nature of the threat is itself
the severity signal. Do not default this category to UNKNOWN severity
just because no injury is mentioned; personal-safety threats are urgent
by nature when described as currently happening.
